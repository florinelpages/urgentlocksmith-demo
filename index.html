FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

How quickly will a locksmith arrive?
Our average national arrival time is 20–25 minutes from the moment you call. In dense urban areas like Greater London or Manchester, this is often faster. We dispatch the closest available locksmith to your postcode automatically.
How much does an emergency call-out cost?
Our prices start from £59 during core hours (09:00–17:00, Monday to Friday). Out-of-hours, weekend and complex jobs are quoted upfront before any work starts — there are no hidden fees and no call-out charge.
Are your locksmiths vetted and insured?
Yes. Every engineer in our network is DBS-checked, police-vetted, fully insured and qualified to fit British Standard BS3621 locks that meet UK home insurance requirements.
Will my door be damaged?
In the vast majority of lockout cases we use non-destructive entry techniques — your lock and door stay intact. If destructive entry is unavoidable (e.g. a broken cylinder), we explain the cost of a replacement before proceeding.
Do you provide a warranty?
Yes — every job comes with a 12-month warranty covering both parts and workmanship. If anything fails within that window, we return free of charge.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major debit and credit cards, contactless payments, bank transfer and cash. Payment is taken on completion of the work — never upfront.
